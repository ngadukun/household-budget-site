**NestEgg** is a simple, beautiful household budget planner that lives entirely in your browser. Track monthly income and spending, set limits per category, keep recurring bills in check, and watch your savings goals grow — no account, no server, no data leaving your device.

## Features

- **Monthly summary** — income, spending, money left to spend, and savings progress at a glance, with month-by-month navigation.
- **Budget categories** — set a monthly limit per category and watch progress bars warn you before you overshoot.
- **Transactions** — add, search, filter and delete income and expenses.
- **Hand-drawn charts** — a donut chart of spending by category and a 6-month spending trend bar chart, both drawn with plain SVG (no chart library).
- **Savings goals** — track targets like an emergency fund or a vacation, and top them up in one tap.
- **Recurring bills** — due dates, paid/overdue status, and a running total of what's still due.
- **Quick insights** — biggest cost, daily average, over-budget warnings, and your closest goal.
- **Local persistence** — everything is saved in `localStorage`; a realistic sample household is preloaded so it looks alive on first open, and you can reset to it any time.
- **Responsive** — clean layout on desktop and phone.

## Use it

Just open `index.html` in any modern browser — there is no build step and no dependency to install.
